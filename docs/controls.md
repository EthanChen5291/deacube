# DeaCube controls

Every action and its input. The in-game shortcut sheet (`?`) shows the same keys with pictures.

| Action | Input |
|---|---|
| Start | The menu: continue (newest save or autosave), new song (type a vibe or roll the dice), gallery (demo songs: hover a card to hear its chorus, click to open it playing), learn (a guided first song), quit. Move the pointer over the wall of cubes to play it |
| Menu | The logo cube (top left) or Esc opens the paper strip: resume, new song, save, load, learn, settings (fullscreen, camera follow, captions), clear song (hold), main menu. Undo and redo sit beside the logo |
| Place islands | The deck at the bottom (or I): the next chords, the chords of your key, a few colour chords, dice, duplicate, the wand (place the rest), a Moon card, a keyboard card, a stairs card and a melody card. Each card is its chord's weather (sunny, dreamy, rainy, moonlit, stormy, spicy, floaty) with its job number (1 home, a house · 2 away, a flag · 3 heart · 4 pull, a curling arrow); a star marks the cards that fit the pattern in your hand, or the island you are looking at. Hover a card for its words; the islands doing the same job show ⇄: those chords can swap. Click a card to add a column after the selected island, or drag it: onto an empty slot above or below an island to stack it in that column, between columns to insert, against an island's side to merge |
| Edit an island | Hover it with empty hands: a ribbon appears on its front edge with five things — move (or grab its edge: drag it anywhere — onto another column to join it, between two columns for a new one, left is earlier), the speaker (on/off), repeat (×1 → ×2 → ×3 → ×4: a conveyor belt, one slot per pass, that stays at its last slot until the song loops; every island of a column at ×2 plays the column twice; from ×2, rest the pointer on repeat for its − (one pass fewer), rewind (it unwinds in place instead) and vary (each pass varies a little)), copy (a new column after it) and ⋯. The ⋯ card holds everything else on an even grid: ▼ ▲ (an octave lower or higher: on its turn a raised island rises like a tower out of the sea and stays up until the song loops, then sinks; a lowered one dips), extend (→: ×1 → ×2 → ×3 → off: the island and the next one to three islands of its section join into one long grid, a thin line at each chord change, and its cubes walk on through it left to right, their pattern re-voiced for each chord; "fill the section" extends it to the section's end), launch (↗ or Shift+→: a cymbal swell into the next section and a crash on its first beat), lead (the spotlight), copy below (a layer below), copy paths / paste paths, paste here, then chord, mood, drum fill, climb, energy, length and the trash can (delete). The dotted islands above and below add an empty layer with the same chord. With a cube or a copied pattern in your hand the ribbon and the dotted islands stay away, so every click goes to the tiles; they return when your hand is empty. The island's triangle fills as its column plays |
| Quiet grids | A grid with no cubes, and a grid whose cubes repeat the grid before it in its lane (the same instruments, tiles and lengths: it wears a small repeat mark, like %, on its front corner), rest flat and a step paler so the bars where something new happens stand out. They stand up again under the pointer, whenever a cube or a pattern is in your hand, and while their column plays. Only the look changes |
| Hear notes | With empty hands, rest the pointer on a tile to hear it, or click it: a click plays the note louder than anything the song plays (the song dips under it). Press a tile and drag to move its island |
| Draw a cube's path | Pick up a cube: click an instrument cube on the left (or press 1–9, 0); hover it a moment to fan out the sounds of its group (koto, shamisen, flute, 808 kit …) and click one. The cube rides your cursor at the size of the next note. Size first: pick the size, then place the note (the size row — beside your cube, over the grid while you draw —, the wheel over a grid, [ and ], . for dotted; each change plays the length). Click a tile: a hologram cube starts your path and every note plays as you place it. Keep clicking: hover a tile to hear the notes a click would add (a far tile of the same grid fills the line to it), the same tile again is the same note again, a drag paints notes. R (or the hollow cube on the row) adds a rest of the chosen size, before the first note too. The melody never hits a wall: when it outgrows its grid it walks on onto the next grid of its lane, still one cube with one path (a lane with no grid there gets an empty one; at a section's last chord the grid grows a measure while the section has room; past the section a new path starts by itself on the next section's grid, and past the song's end a chord is added). Each note lands on the grid that plays at its time: once the melody has walked on, a click on an earlier grid lands on the same tile of the current one (both flash). The beat dots along each grid's front edge fill as you go. Backspace or Cmd/Ctrl+Z take notes back one at a time (and give back what the melody made for itself). Right-click, Enter, the ✓, Esc, a click on the sea or on another grid end the path and always keep its notes: the cube turns solid and a new cube stays in your hand (Esc again, a right-click on the sea or its instrument cube puts it down). One undo takes back the whole path from pen down to pen up. With a cube in your hand a small + lies by the last note of every finished path of that group: click it to pick the path up again and go on (or Backspace) from its end. A stopped song stays silent while you draw: Space (or ▶⟲ on the row) loops the grids the path covers; a playing song keeps playing, looping those grids, and plays on when you finish |
| Keyboard | The keyboard card adds a keyboard island: two octaves of piano keys from the key's home note. With a cube in hand a click adds exactly that key (leaps are fine), the same key again repeats it, a drag plays a run, R adds a rest; keys that fit the part's chord carry a dot, the home key a house. A melody longer than its measure walks on onto the next keyboard of its lane (the same key there; one is added when there is none), like a path on a grid, and ends the same ways (right-click, Enter, ✓, Esc, a click elsewhere: the notes stay). A note on the lowest or highest key adds an octave on that side (up to five octaves); octaves the finished melody does not use fold away again, and + octave below / above in its ⋯ card add one by hand (fit to the melody trims it). A few floating spheres, one per voice in its cube's colour, play its notes, bouncing from key to key and resting on the last key in the gaps. With empty hands it plays the keys you press |
| Copy and paste | Cmd/Ctrl+C (or copy in a cube card's ⋯) copies its pattern; the clipboard chip (bottom right) shows it. Cmd/Ctrl+V or "paste → next grid" drops it on the next grid, or click the chip and then any grid (a ghost shows the notes first), or drag the chip onto a grid: the pattern is re-voiced for that chord and appears there. The chip's fan (and the cube card) paste it changed: Cmd+↑ / Cmd+↓ an echo an octave higher or lower (on the beat or an eighth late), Cmd+Alt+↑ / Cmd+Alt+↓ a step up or down the scale, Cmd+Shift+V an answer (the same pattern, its last note coming home to the chord) |
| Cube or sphere | The switch above the instrument column picks the shape of the next piece. A cube steps through every tile on its way (a far click fills the line). A sphere leaps: a click on any tile of the grid adds just that tile, the hover shows the bounce as an arc, and in the song it bounces there, higher and longer the farther it goes, rolling as it flies |
| Copy a whole grid | Copy paths in a grid's ⋯ card (or Cmd/Ctrl+C over one of its empty tiles) copies every path on it, octave copies and harmonies included. On any other grid, paste paths (×n) in its ⋯ card or Cmd/Ctrl+V pastes them all at once, each re-voiced for that grid's chord the way a single paste is (one undo) |
| Cube ideas | In a cube card's ⋯, or with the pointer on a cube: Shift+↑ / Shift+↓ copies it an octave higher or lower as a glass layer above the grid or in its reflection below (up to two octaves each way), Alt+↑ / Alt+↓ adds a harmony a fourth or a fifth above or below that keeps clear of the other cubes' notes (hover the button to hear it first), U turns its path upside down |
| Edit a cube | Hover it (a rounded outline marches around it) and click: it flies up beside its card. The card shows only its notes and how to change them: the instrument chip (click: pick a sound, right-click: mute, hold: solo), volume, the flat path grid, the rhythm strip, the size row and a trash can (deletes the cube; undo brings it back). With no note selected: the size row picks the size of the next note, a click on a free tile adds it at the end, the hollow cube adds a rest; on the grid, drag a bead to move it, click a bead for a rest, right-click removes it, drag the start ring to shift the path. Click a note on the strip to select it: a click on any tile now inserts a note right after it (that one becomes the selected note, so clicks keep inserting in order; its bold right edge shows where), the size row sets its length, ▲ ▼ move it a step higher or lower, the scissors (or S) split it into two halves, R (or right-click) makes it a rest, its trash can (or Backspace) deletes it, dragging its right edge stretches it. A note that no longer fits its grid walks on onto the next grid of its lane, as while drawing (a section's last grid grows a measure). A melody that runs through several grids shows one grid at a time, in that grid's colour: the one that holds the selected note; click another part of the strip to see that grid. ⋯ holds everything else as icons: redraw, reverse, the path modes · copy, paste → next grid, flip, climb, octave copies, harmonies · twin, shadow, echo, stamp, octave, gate. Delete (or the trash can) deletes the cube; Backspace never does. Esc or ✕ sends it back |
| The loop while editing | Inspecting a cube loops its column so you hear the change in context (drawing a path: Space, see above); Esc, a click on the sea or the ✕ on the timeline's loop badge ends it and puts the transport back |
| The timeline (top centre) | One row: a pill per section in its letter's colour, as wide as the time it plays; in a small song each pill also shows its measures as plain cells in their chords' colours. The pill the song is in is the bright one, and the bar under it fills as it plays. Click a pill (or a cell) to go there: the camera frames it and Play starts from it (a playing song plays on from there); right-click only moves the song position. The curl at the end of the row turns the song loop on and off |
| Sections | Every four measures make a section, lettered on its plinth in a circle of the letter's own colour (sections with the same chords share a letter and its colour) and shown on the timeline as a pill of the same colour with the same letter; a section sparkles when it fills. Hover a plinth for its header — four things: the letter (click it to name the section: intro, verse, chorus, bridge, drop, outro), copy, the trash can (delete) and ⋯ (split here, join with the next, drums — a Moon for this section — and a build-up into the next section). A column dropped inside a section joins it |
| Stairs | The stairs card adds a staircase grid: one step per note, falling or climbing into the next chord, the grids after it standing on its height. Its ribbon flips the direction (falling / climbing); its ⋯ card picks the kind (hover each to hear it; a star marks the one that fits the chord), the number of steps (3 to 8), the speed (as cube sizes), a lead-in, the octave and repeats; with a cube in hand a click on a step makes that cube the runner |
| Melody roll | The melody card adds a melody roll behind the column you are on: a piano roll in the colours of the chords it plays over, a dot on each safe note. With a cube in hand click or drag across its cells to place notes at the chosen size (right-click erases); drawing past its end grows it (½ → 1 → 2 → 4 → 8 measures, never across a section). Its ⋯ card sets the length (or auto: as long as its notes), doubles it (the melody twice), switches between eighth and sixteenth cells and sets the octave; repeat is on its ribbon |
| Select many grids | Shift+drag a rectangle across grids (or Shift+click a grid, or a section's plinth for the whole section): they lift and shimmer, and a bar offers four things: copy, paste after, repeat and the trash can (Cmd+C, Cmd+V and Delete work too; Shift+↑ / Shift+↓ move them an octave, Shift+→ toggles a build-up into the next section). Esc or a click on the sea clears it |
| Drums | A Moon holds the kit rows; its dice rolls a new groove. A Moon plays from the part it sits under until the next Moon starts, gliding along with the song (dotted tracks on the sea show each drum part; a part with no Moon has no drums). N or the Moon card adds a Moon at the part you are on; drag a Moon sideways to move where it starts |
| Melody spotlight | While a song plays (and in present mode) the stage dims and every grid carrying the melody gets a cone of light in the measures it sounds. The spotlight in a chord grid's ⋯ card marks it as the lead; keyboards, staircases and melody rolls always are. The "lights" switch beside the present button turns it off |
| Nudge a note | On a cube's card, select a note and press ▲ higher or ▼ lower (they show beside the size row while a note is selected): that one note moves a step up or down the key, only on that tile, without changing the chord |
| Play | The big play button or Space (hold the button to stop and rewind), Home stops, L loops, M metronome; drag or tap the tempo digits; the knob opens the sound drawer (swing, key, tone, space, weather, volume); scroll over an instrument cube for its volume |
| Live punch-ins | Hold J stutter, K funnel, H half-time, B drop, E spotlight (while playing; keys only — there are no pads on screen) |
| Present | P or the sticker in the top right: the islands rise from the sea as their columns play while the camera slowly descends. Space pauses, Esc or P returns |
| Camera | Drag the sea to pan, right-drag orbits, wheel zooms toward the cursor, two-finger sideways scroll or Shift+wheel pans, WASD / arrow keys pan, Tab / Shift+Tab next / previous island, click an island to frame it, O frames everything, hold Q / E to orbit, C resets the view, F follows the playing column |
| Undo / redo, save / load | Cmd/Ctrl+Z, Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Z, Cmd/Ctrl+S, Cmd/Ctrl+O, or the menu strip. The main menu writes an autosave (never over your save); starting a new song keeps a backup of the old one |
| Shortcuts | ? (or "shortcuts" in the menu strip) opens a sheet of every key, each with its picture |
