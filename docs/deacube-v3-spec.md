## 2. Package A — "Edit": hover outline, fly-to-camera inspector, flat path grid

Owned files: `Assets/AudioCube.cs`, `Assets/PathManager.cs`, `Assets/UIManager.cs`, `Assets/TileInteraction.cs`, `Assets/DeaCube/UIKit.cs`, `Assets/DeaCube/CubeInspector.cs` (replace stub), new `Assets/DeaCube/PathGridView.cs`, new `Assets/DeaCube/CubeOutline.cs`, new `Assets/Resources/Shaders/DeaCube_MarchingEdge.shader`, new `Assets/DeaCube/Tests/V3ChecksA.cs`.

### 2.1 Hover-near outline
- Candidate cube = the cube under the cursor ray, OR the top cube resting on the tile under the ray, OR (when the ray hits no free tile) the finalized cube whose projected screen rect (8 renderer-bounds corners) expanded by 16 px contains the cursor (nearest rect centre wins). Never while drawing, over UI, WorldLocked, or during an island drag. When the ray hits a FREE tile, that tile wins (its hover highlight shows, no outline): what is highlighted is what a click does.
- `CubeOutline` (one shared instance, retargeted): a wire box 1.22 × the cube, 12 edges built as thin quads (0.035 u) with UV.x = distance along the edge; shader `DeaCube/MarchingEdge` (URP unlit, additive, ZTest LEqual, no ZWrite): dashes 0.09 u long marching along every edge at 0.6 u/s, corners brighter (bracket look), colour = lerp(cube colour, white, 0.45), soft pulse ±15 %. Follows the cube's transform every frame (incl. squash, hop). In: scale 1.45 → 1.22 with OutBack 0.18 s + fade; out: fade 0.12 s. Hover-in sound: soft tick, pitch by instrument, ≤ 4/s. `Onboarding.Notify(Ev.CubeHovered)` once per hover.

### 2.2 Click → inspect (the fly)
- Left click (press + release < 6 px) on the candidate cube → `CubeInspector.Open(cube)`. A drag that starts on a cube does nothing. Alt/Option + press on a tile that carries a cube starts a new path there (stacking). Right-click a cube = delete (v2).
- Open: `PathManager.Select(cube, true)` (so every UIManager binding uses `Sel`); `WorldInput.Lock("inspector")`; `cube.SetWorldVisible(false, true)` (body hidden, path line + beads stay); a proxy (rounded-box mesh, instrument colour, emission) spawns at the cube's world pose and flies 0.55 s along a Bezier (start → start + up 1.6 u → target) to a camera-anchored point: viewport (0.28, 0.52) at 3.0 u, scale so it spans ~24 % of the screen height; rotation slerps to a hero pose (tilt 24°, yaw 38°) plus one extra full spin about the up axis (InOutSine); position OutCubic; whoosh + a short trail. A dim quad parented to the camera at 3.6 u (behind the proxy) fades to rgba(0.02,0.01,0.05,0.62) in 0.35 s (a UI dim would cover the 3D proxy). The inspector card slides in from the right (0.35 s OutCubic).
- While open: the proxy turns slowly (12°/s) and floats (±0.05 u, 0.6 Hz); dragging on it spins it with inertia; it squashes + flashes on every landing of its cube (`AudioCube.OnLanded`); instrument change recolours it with a burst; volume scales its glow; mute greys it.
- Close (Esc, card close button, click on the dim area outside card and proxy, right-click on the dim): the proxy flies back (0.45 s) to the cube's CURRENT rest position (re-targeted every frame), shrinking to world size; the card slides out; the dim fades; on arrival: destroy proxy, `SetWorldVisible(true)`, land thud + ripple, `Unlock("inspector")`, `PathManager.Deselect()`. Invariant: `PathManager.selectedCube != null` ⇔ the inspector is open (or opening/closing).
- Delete from the card: the proxy pops (scale-out + burst), the cube is deleted, the inspector closes without a return flight.
- Opening another cube while open: close then open (no double proxies). `Onboarding.Notify(Ev.CubeInspected / Ev.InspectorClosed)`.

### 2.3 The inspector card (right side)
Anchored right-centre, 28 px margin, width ~560 px, glass panel; it hosts EVERYTHING the v2 cube panel had (re-parent or rebuild UIManager's cube panel rows: mute, volume, spotlight hold, delete, 10 role swatches, step lengths, NecklaceDial, octave, twin (hold: variants), shadow, echo (hold: shimmer), rider, gates, modes, stamp) plus: a close button, the flat path grid (§2.4), a path-tools row (redraw pencil, reverse, shift ← → ↑ ↓ (moves the whole path one cell when every node stays inside the grid), path mode loop/ping-pong/once). The old bottom-right cube panel no longer appears on its own. Keep all v2 cube hotkeys (T, R, W, ↑/↓, [ ], - =, G, E) acting on the inspected cube; number keys 1-9, 0 recolour it. Register hint targets `inspector`, `inspector.grid`, `inspector.necklace`, `inspector.swatches`, `inspector.close`, `inspector.volume`.

### 2.4 PathGridView (flat, editable path)
- Cells = the cube's island grid: cols × rows (Moon: 6 × 4 kit grid). x → left→right, row z → bottom→top. Cell: rounded square in the tile's colour, the tile's role glyph (disc root, triangles third, square fifth, star seventh, ring extension; kit glyphs on Moons), brightness by pitch. Cell size ~58 px.
- Overlay: segments between consecutive nodes (5 px, cube colour, a chevron at each midpoint pointing along the path); beads: normal filled, rest hollow, ghost small, accent large, sticker glyph for ratchet/coin/tie/lift; node 0 = bigger ring + start dot; Loop → faint dashed closing segment last→first; PingPong → double chevrons at both ends; Once → end cap. Repeated cells: beads fan out slightly per visit. The bead of the node the cube is on now pulses (TryGetPose).
- Gestures (every committed change: setters/SetPath on the cube, then `History.Push()` on release; notes preview through `TileInteraction.PlayPreview`, which obeys the v2 preview rules):
  - click empty cell → append a node (any cell; non-adjacent is legal for playback);
  - drag from the LAST bead across cells → extend (each entered cell appended, consecutive duplicates ignored);
  - drag any other bead → move that node (live, commit on release; its sticker moves with it);
  - click a bead → toggle rest; hold 0.35 s → sticker popover (none, rest, ghost, accent, ratchet2, ratchet3, coin, tie, lift) next to the bead;
  - right-click a bead → remove the node (at least one node stays);
  - pencil tool → the next press clears the path to that cell and dragging appends (release ends);
  - reverse → node order and stickers reversed; shift arrows as §2.3.
  - `mods`, `rests`, `gridX/gridZ` must stay aligned with `nodes` on insert/move/remove (AudioCube.SyncLists pads/truncates only at the end — do the list surgery yourself).
- `Onboarding.Notify(Ev.GridEdited)` on the first committed edit per open; `Ev.RhythmEdited` when the necklace/step changes.

### 2.5 AudioCube / world visuals
Keep all v2 behaviour. Add whatever the outline/inspector need. `SetWorldVisible(bool body, bool path)` (M0) must hide/show body, halo, glyph overlays, ghost cubes, echo quads, octave disc (body) and the line + beads (path).

### 2.6 Path drawing (M0 already fixed — A keeps it and may polish)
Bug: a click on a tile then a click on its neighbour finished the path on release (the extension press set `dragging`), so clicking along a line made a new cube every two tiles, and fast drags skipped tiles. Fixed model: a press on a free tile starts a path; a release finishes it only if that stroke dragged across at least one more tile; plain clicks keep drawing (click-mode); a click on the last tile, Enter or right-click finishes; Esc cancels; a click on a non-adjacent tile of the same island fills the straight/diagonal grid line to it; fast drags fill the tiles they skipped; a press on another island or empty space finishes. A finished path does not select its cube (drawing the next path stays one click away).

### 2.7 M0 AudioCube API (for C and A)
`struct CubePose { bool active; int window; int step; float stepFrac; float stepBeat; TileInteraction tile; Hit hit; int nextStep; float nextBeat; TileInteraction nextTile; Hit nextHit; }` and `bool TryGetPose(out CubePose p)` (pure read of the same math UpdatePlayback uses: active window at the current song beat, step k + progress, Decide for k, next pattern hit inside the window); `int PassFor(int w, int loopsAhead)`; `void StepAt(float localBeat, out int k, out float progress)`; `static event Action<AudioCube,int,int,Hit> OnLanded` (cube, window, step, hit) raised by Land for hit steps; `void SetWorldVisible(bool body, bool path)`.

### 3.2 Song start
`SongManager.StartFreshSong(SongData generated)`: builds ONLY measure 0 (centred on the origin) plus the demo Moon with its default groove (v2 AddDemoMoon + the three Moon cubes; no Keys/Bass demo cubes), stopped (not playing), camera framing island 0 and the Moon, History reset; `IslandTray.SetDeck(measures[1..])`. ### 3.3 Merge and split
- Data (M0 fields): `group` (int, 0 = none). Invariant, enforced by `SongManager.NormalizeGroups()` after every structural edit: members of one group are consecutive in Route order and laid out left→right in that order, platforms touching (member j+1's px = member j's px + member j's Width), same pz (front rows aligned); a group of one is dissolved (group 0).
- Merge gesture: while dragging an island (IslandDrag) or a tray ghost, if its platform's west edge comes within 1.4 u of another island's east edge (or east edge near a west edge) with ≥ 40 % z-overlap → merge preview: the target edge shows a vertical light sheet, the dragged island is pulled magnetically toward its slot, a rising shimmer plays. Only the outer ends of a group are targets. Release → `SongManager.MergeIslands(moving, target, eastSide)`: the moving island moves in the song order to right after (east) / right before (west) the target group, gets the group id (new id = max + 1), positions re-laid, RebuildFromState with the v2 seek-keeping rules, History.Push, then the merge animation.
- Merge animation: the moving member starts 0.8 u out on its side and slides in (0.28 s OutBack); a white flash sweeps along the seam back→front (0.35 s); sparkles along the seam; both platforms pulse; each member's outline draws itself in over 0.4 s; sound: a two-note "zip" (Sparkle + the two roots) — no chord clash while playing (use UI sounds only while playing). `Onboarding.Notify(Ev.IslandsMerged)`.
- Look of a group ("the divide between the chords"): every member keeps its own platform colour; a seam bridge (0.6 u box filling the rounded-corner notch, colour = blend of both platforms) joins them; a glowing border line runs around EACH member's top rim (LineRenderer loop, width 0.06, the member's chord colour, additive, a slow flow) — where two members touch, their two lines run side by side and read as the chord divide. Singletons keep the v2 look.
- Moving: a platform drag on any member moves the whole group rigidly. A HUB drag on a member tears it out (spark line along the seam, reversed zip) and continues as a normal island drag. Hovering a seam shows a small scissors glyph at its front end; clicking it splits the group there (the right part slides 1.6 u away). `Onboarding.Notify(Ev.IslandSplit)`.
- Music: members keep their own chord, bars, cubes and windows; the group plays in order without pauses; paths cannot cross a seam (Adjacent requires the same island). Route draws no cable between members of a group; the comet crosses a seam with a low glide (0.35 u above the platform) during the last beat instead of the high arc (Route.PositionAlong returns that path).

### 3.4 Camera: island based, no auto-chase, low friction
- `followPlayhead` defaults to false (scene too). `F` toggles "island follow": when on, the camera glides (0.7 s) to the lit island only when the lit island CHANGES (discrete); never a continuous leash. When off the camera never moves by itself.
- Pan = grab-the-ground 1:1 with NO smoothing lag while dragging; release → inertia (velocity from the last ~0.08 s, exponential decay τ = 0.35 s, stop < 0.05 u/s). Orbit direct while dragging, yaw inertia τ = 0.3 s. Wheel zoom ×0.88 / ×1.14 per notch toward the cursor, lerp rate 16/s. Horizontal trackpad scroll (`mouseScrollDelta.x`) pans sideways; Shift + wheel pans forward/back. Soft bounds: pad 60 u, spring 0.8 s, only when not dragging and more than 2 u outside.
- Keys (not typing, not WorldLocked): WASD / arrow keys pan (speed 0.9 × distance per second, 0.12 s ramp); Tab / Shift+Tab next / previous island (glide + frame); Q home; O frame all; F follow toggle. (A/D no longer step islands; ↑/↓ octave only exists while inspecting, when the world is locked.)
- Island focus: a click on an island's platform or hub (press + release < 6 px, no tile/cube under the cursor) selects it (pill) and glides to frame it (group aware); double-click frames tighter. Tray placement glides to the new island.
- `Suspended` (M0): LateUpdate does nothing (the Presenter / menu drive the camera). `SaveView()/RestoreView()` (M0 real) must keep working.

## 4. Package C — "Present": the waterfall

Owned files: `Assets/DeaCube/Presenter.cs` (replace stub; split into more new files under `Assets/DeaCube/Present/` if useful), new shaders under `Assets/Resources/Shaders/DeaCube_Present*.shader`, new `Assets/DeaCube/Tests/V3ChecksC.cs`. Read-only: everything else.

### 4.1 Principle: time is depth
The song becomes a descending cascade. The camera sinks at a constant rate K per beat (K = 0.42 u/beat; 0.84 u/s at 120 BPM). Every landing is a cube rolling off one tile and dropping onto the next tile, which has just fallen into place a little lower; tiles fall away after their last use, so the song pours downward like a waterfall. The grids you edit ARE the choreography: a cube's path is the route it rolls, its rhythm is when it drops, and each island's fall pattern (the new measure-bar button, `KeyBlock.fall` 0..5) orders how the rest of its tiles fall. Audio is untouched (the AudioCubes keep scheduling; the Presenter is visual only) and landings coincide with the notes.

### 4.2 Stage
- Built on Enter on layer `DeaLayers.Present`; the main camera's culling mask = Present only (UI overlay is unaffected); a deep indigo backdrop + fog; nothing of the edit world is visible.
- Terraces: island i in Route order (start beat S_i, width W_i) → terrace top height Y_i = −K·S_i, horizontal position H_{i+1} = H_i + d̂·(W_i/2 + W_{i+1}/2 + 1.2), d̂ = normalize(1, 0, −0.55): a staircase that descends to the right and toward the camera. Merged groups sit edge to edge (one strip). Moon(s): one companion terrace that travels with the current island terrace (offset behind it) and descends with the same law.
- Proxy tiles (pooled): same rounded-box mesh and colour as the edit tiles, flat (NO pitch rise — in the cascade height means time; pitch shows as brightness and the flash), role glyph decal. Proxy performer cube per AudioCube (instrument colour, emission). Only terraces in [current − 1, current + 2] exist; loops continue the staircase (B = loopIndex · TotalBeats + songBeat keeps growing).

### 4.3 Motion laws
- Landing schedule per cube from the public API (M0): windows, StepsInWindow, StepStartLocalBeat, Decide(w, k, PassFor(w, loops)), TileAt(w, node). A landing = hit step (hit.hit): beat b, tile t, weight, mod, ratchet, tie.
- Tile t landed at beat b must be at height −K·b at b: it DROPS there starting δ = min(0.3 beat, 0.18 s) before b (gravity ease, tiny bounce). A tile landed on again later drops again (always lower). A tile's last landing in the window → after the cube leaves it, it falls away (g = 18 u/s², slight tumble, fades 12 u below).
- Free tiles (no landing within the next 2 beats) follow the island's fall pattern: every beat the pattern's next batch (tiles/beats of the window) drops to the current level −K·b, so the terrace keeps pace in a visible wave; at the window end the remaining tiles pour away in pattern order over the last beat. Patterns: 0 auto (island index picks 1..5), 1 wave → (columns left→right), 2 rows (front→back), 3 spiral (outside→in), 4 rain (seeded random), 5 bloom (centre→out).
- Arrival: the next terrace's tiles fall into formation from 6 u above, in pattern order, during the 1.5 beats before its start (the previous terrace's pour visually continues into it).
- Cube: rests on its tile (squash on landing: tile flash in the instrument colour, splash ring, short upward light streak; accents bigger, ghosts small, rests press without flash, ratchets = extra mini-bounces on the same tile, ties = no flash). Moving to the next landing on another tile: in the last min(85 % of the interval, 0.55 s) it tips over the leading bottom edge (roll 90°) and then drops ballistically (continuing the tumble to a flat pose) onto the lower tile, arriving exactly at the landing beat; intervals < 0.16 s use a quick slide-drop with one 90° turn. Same tile: the tile drops under the cube and the cube falls with it. First landing on a terrace: the cube falls in from 5 u above. After its last landing in a window: a Rider falls to its first tile on the next terrace (long drop, exact arrival); a resident rolls off the edge and falls away with the pour.
- Camera: target = the current terrace centre (C² smooth blend across transitions), height −K·B + 7, pitch 38°, yaw −28°, distance 17 u, idle orbit ±6° over 20 s, mouse parallax ±3°, critically damped 0.6 s. No cuts.

### 4.4 UX
- Enter: `P` or the transport "present" button (M0). HUD fades out (`UIManager.SetHudVisible(false)`), `WorldInput.Lock("present", true)`, `OrbitCamera.I.SaveView(); Suspended = true`, stage builds, the song seeks to 0, island 0's tiles assemble over 1.5 beats, then `GlobalClock.Play()`. `Onboarding.Notify(Ev.PresentEntered)`.
- On screen: the cascade, a thin progress line at the bottom, an exit ✕ (top-right) that fades in on mouse move and out after 2 s idle. Space pauses/resumes; Esc / P / ✕ exits.
- Exit: 0.4 s fade, destroy the stage, restore culling mask, `Suspended = false; RestoreView()`, HUD, `Unlock("present")`, pause playback. `Onboarding.Notify(Ev.PresentExited)`.
